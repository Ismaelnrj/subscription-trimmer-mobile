# Trimio posting calendar, first 30 days

Phase 2 of the growth plan. The job of this month is not growth, it is finding
which of four angles the algorithm rewards, cheaply, before any money goes
into the paid track.

## The three decisions this is built on

**German first.** The founder is in Vienna, the app ships verified DACH prices,
and the Play listing has been German since 2026-09-10. TikTok and Reels serve
locally, so German content reaches the market Trimio is actually strongest in,
and it can be spoken natively on camera. English is a repost where a clip is
language light, not a parallel production line.

**Four posts a week, shot in one sitting.** A solo operator posting daily quits
in week three. Four is sustainable, and batching means one filming session
rather than four interruptions. Wednesday and Sunday are the natural batch days
because they leave the rest of the week for the app.

**Nothing paid until something organic proves itself.** The growth plan
sequences this deliberately: boost a clip that already worked, then open an
always on UAC test, never both from a standing start. A post that clears
roughly 10,000 views on its own is the signal to spend behind it.

---

## The four pillars

| Pillar | Job | Reach | Converts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Stop the scroll. Nobody searches for a subscription tracker, they search for relief | Highest | Low |
| **Knowledge** | Be findable. "Netflix kündigen" is searched every day, forever | Evergreen | Medium |
| **Product** | Show the thing. Only meaningful once someone already cares | Low | Highest |
| **Founder** | Be a person. Solo builders get latitude that companies do not | Medium | Medium |

Roughly 2 pain, 1 knowledge, 1 product per week, with founder posts folded in
where they fit. Front load pain, because reach compounds and conversion
without reach is nothing.

---

## Week 1, establish the problem

**Mon, pain.** The hook from the shot list, filmed straight to camera.
> Du zahlst für sieben Abos. Du erinnerst dich an vier.

Hold the number on screen. No app footage at all. This one is a test of the
line itself, and app footage would confound the result.

**Wed, product.** The paste an email beat, exactly as the shot list frames it.
> Abo hinzufügen in vier Sekunden. Bestätigungsmail einfügen, fertig.

**Fri, knowledge.** Genuinely useful, no product mention until the last second.
> Was diese fünf Abos wirklich kosten, Stand September 2026

**This was drafted as "these five raised their prices in 2026" and that claim
cannot be supported.** The catalogue rows were corrected on 2026-09-05, which
is the catalogue being fixed rather than evidence a provider raised anything:
the figures were written in July 2026 and some were simply wrong. Netflix
Standard and Amazon Prime needed no change at all. Stating an increase would be
precisely the overclaim `isPriceFresh` exists to stop the app making, and the
marketing has no licence the product refuses itself.

A current price post is honest, just as searchable, and ages into an increase
post naturally once there is a second dated reading to compare against.

Use only the five DACH rows carrying a verified date. **Xbox Game Pass Ultimate
is 22.99 USD on the GLOBAL row**, not a euro price, so it does not belong in a
German list whatever the calendar first said.

**Sun, founder.** Phone camera, no edit.
> Ich habe Trimio gebaut, weil ich vergessen habe, ein Abo zu kündigen. Ein Jahr lang.

## Week 2, be findable

**Mon, knowledge.** The highest intent search in this category.
> So kündigst du Netflix, Spotify und Disney+ wirklich

Three cancellations, real screens, twenty seconds. Trimio appears once at the
end as the thing that reminds you before the next one.

**Wed, pain.** The specific beats the general.
> Drei Jahre Fitnessstudio. Zweimal dort gewesen.

**Fri, product.** The calendar view, which is the most screenshot friendly
screen in the app.
> Alle Abbuchungen des Monats auf einem Bildschirm.

**Sun, pain, English.** First English test, deliberately language light so it
can be reshot cheaply if the audience is not there.
> The most expensive app on your phone is the one you forgot about.

---

## Week 3, proof and the ask

**Mon, knowledge.** Positioning disguised as advice, which is the only kind
that travels.
> Warum ich keine App mit meinem Bankkonto verbinde

This is the privacy argument, and it is Trimio's strongest differentiator
because most competitors cannot make it.

**Wed, pain.** Numbers people recognise.
> 47 € im Monat. Für Dinge, die ich nicht nutze.

**Fri, product, referral.** The first explicit ask of the month.
> Einen Monat verschenken, einen Monat bekommen.

Give the referral its own post rather than tacking it onto another. It is a
real offer and it reads as an afterthought when bolted on.

**Sun, founder.** Build in public, honestly.
> Was ich in vier Wochen gelernt habe, als Einzelperson eine App zu vermarkten

---

## Week 4, double down

By now two or three posts will have outperformed the rest. This week is not
new ideas, it is variations on whatever worked.

**Mon.** Reshoot the best performing hook with a different opening frame.
**Wed.** The same idea in the other language.
**Fri.** The comment you got most often, answered as its own post. Comments are
the cheapest content research there is and the only kind that is already
validated.
**Sun.** Month one numbers, publicly. Installs, what worked, what did not.

---

## Rules that apply to every post

**The copy rules are the same as the store listing.** No dash as clause
separating punctuation. Captions in the middle band, since TikTok and Reels
cover the bottom 20% and right 15%. Soft Mint never behind white text: navy
behind white, or `#1F7A62` for type that must read as mint.

**No Apple App Store badge, ever.** There is no iOS build. The link is
`play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trimio.app`

**Every clip has to work muted.** Most viewers never turn sound on, so the
caption carries the whole message or the post does not work.

**Say the same thing the store says.** Someone who sees a clip and taps through
should read the same promise twice: "Wissen, bevor abgebucht wird" and "Know
before you're charged" are the two live taglines.

---

## What to measure, and what to ignore

Watch **watch time to the three second mark** and **profile visits**. The first
says whether the hook works. The second says whether anyone wanted more.

Ignore likes. They correlate with nothing that matters here.

Installs will lag by days and will not attribute cleanly to a post, which is
normal and not worth fighting. Play Console's acquisition report is the honest
number, and PostHog already tracks `landing_play_store_click` for anyone who
routes through the site.

## When to start spending

One post clearing roughly 10,000 views organically. Boost that one. Only after
a boosted clip also performs does the always on UAC test open, against the €200
month the plan allows. Never both at once from a cold start, because two
variables moving together tell you nothing about either.
