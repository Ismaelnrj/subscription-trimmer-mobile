How to cancel Netflix

4 steps, in the order you will see them.

Last checked 11 October 2026. Trimio is not affiliated with Netflix.

  1. Sign in at netflix.com in a browser. The Netflix app sends you to the website for this step.
  2. Select your profile icon (top right) → “Account”.
  3. Under “Membership & Billing”, select “Cancel Membership”.
  4. If Netflix offers a pause instead, choose to continue, then confirm with “Finish Cancellation”.
Open the Netflix cancellation page

Good to know

You keep access until the end of your billing period and get a confirmation email. Deleting the app or signing out does not cancel. If you pay through Google Play, Apple or a TV or mobile provider, cancel there instead.
