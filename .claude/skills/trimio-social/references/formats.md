# Formats other than the reel

Adapted from instagram-agent-skill (MIT, see `../scripts/UPSTREAM.md`) and
rewritten around Trimio's rules. The gate in `../SKILL.md` applies to every
format here: nothing goes out without the owner's yes, naming the platform.

## Carousel

- Use it when the idea is steps to re-read: a cancellation guide, "what 5
  subscriptions cost in a year". One claim is a reel, not a carousel.
- 6 to 10 slides at 1080x1350. Cover: six words or fewer, readable as a grid
  thumbnail, clear of the outer 120 px. One idea per slide, at most 25 words.
  A recap slide people can screenshot. One ask at the end. Slide numbers and
  the handle small on every slide.
- Real screenshots of the app only, never a generated screen. Colours and type
  from `trimio-design`: Ink Navy, white, mint only for good news, Montserrat.
- Alt text on the cover at minimum.

## Story

- 3 to 7 frames: something happening, the content, ONE sticker, the close.
  Put the ask at frame 4, not last.
- The link sticker is the one tappable Play link on Instagram. Use the tagged
  story link in `marketing-links.md` so installs are counted by source.
- Question box answers are hooks in the audience's own words: feed them to
  the next reel.

## Comments on other accounts' posts

- One to three sentences, one idea, something specific: a number, a case the
  post missed, a respectful disagreement. Never "Great post", never emoji only.
- Never pitch Trimio in someone else's comments, and never post comments
  automatically. The owner posts them by hand.

## Replies under Trimio's own posts

- Sort first: keyword, lead, substance, question, support, noise. Answer in
  that order and stop when the value stops.
- Answer the question in the reply itself. A question many people ask becomes
  the next reel ("reply with a reel").
- Hide abusive comments; do not argue with them.

## DMs

- Only to people who moved first: commented, replied to a story, asked.
  No cold DMs and no bulk sending; that gets accounts restricted.
- Deliver what was promised in the first message, then one small question.
  Two follow ups at most (+4 days with something new, +10 days to close).
- Keyword auto replies only through Instagram's own tools.

## Profile

- The name field (30 characters, bold, searched by Instagram) read "Ismael"
  on 2026-11-16. Searched words do the work there, for example
  "Trimio | Subscription Tracker" (29). The owner's call.
- The bio link is Linktree and stays Linktree, the owner's call on
  2026-11-16: a long tagged URL in a bio reads as spam. Count bio taps with
  Linktree's own click numbers. If installs by source are wanted, the tagged
  Play link (`marketing-links.md`, Instagram bio, Play) goes behind a Linktree
  button, where nobody sees it. The tagged website link is counted only in
  PostHog: the site's Play buttons carry no referrer.
- Pin three posts with three jobs: the best proof, the clearest "what it
  does", and a cancellation guide.

## A week

Upstream suggests four to five posts a week and 20 minutes of commenting a day. That
is a full time cadence for a solo owner; treat it as a ceiling, not a target,
and measure against the success bars in `../SKILL.md` before adding volume.

## Audit of what was posted

- Rank by multiple of the account's own median, then by shares and saves per
  reach. Raw views mostly measure follower count.
- No views means the first two seconds failed. Views with no follows means the
  profile failed. Fix the one the numbers point at.
- Say the sample size. Six posts show nothing; thirty can show a pattern.
