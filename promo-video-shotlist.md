# Trimio promo video, 15s vertical

Shot list for the re-cut that replaces `trimio_promo_clean_vertical.mp4`.
That file is retired, not re-edited: it shows the pre-redesign screens,
carries an Apple App Store badge for a build that does not exist, and has a
caption sitting under the platform UI.

Format: 1080x1920, 9:16, 15 seconds, 30 or 60fps. Shot on the owner's own
phone, which runs build 40 and therefore the real navy UI.

---

## Before you record

- **Clean the status bar.** Full battery, wifi on, no notification icons,
  Do Not Disturb on. A stray WhatsApp badge dates the clip and pulls the eye.
- **Use believable data.** Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, a gym, an insurance.
  Not "Test 1" and "asdf". The numbers should look like a real month.
- **Record each screen separately** and longer than you need, roughly 6 to 8
  seconds per screen. Trimming is free, re-shooting is not.
- **Move slowly.** Scroll at about half the speed that feels natural. Fast
  motion reads as a blur at 15 seconds and on a small screen.
- Android capture: Quick Settings, then Screen Record. Record internal audio
  off, you are scoring this in the edit.

## Caption safe zone, the thing that broke the last cut

TikTok and Reels draw their own UI over your frame: roughly the **bottom 20%**
(caption, username, music ticker) and the **right 15%** (like, comment, share).
Anything you put there is covered on at least one platform.

**Keep every caption between 25% and 75% of frame height.** Vertically centred
text is safe on every platform at once. The old cut put its closing line low,
which is why it collided.

## Colour rules for overlays

From the brand palette, measured not guessed:

- White text goes on **Ink Navy `#142B3A`** only. That is about 12:1, safe.
- **Never** put white text on Soft Mint. It measures 2.1:1 and fails every
  floor there is.
- Text that must read as mint uses the deepened **`#1F7A62`**, not `#55C6A3`.
- Use mint as an underline, a dot, or a thin rule. It marks things. It never
  carries them.
- Behind captions over app footage, use a navy scrim at about 70% opacity
  rather than a drop shadow. Cleaner, and it keeps contrast predictable.

### Why this order

The hook is a number, not a feature. "You remember 4" is the only line whose
job is to make someone not scroll, and it works because it is uncomfortable
and true. Everything after it is proof.

The paste-email beat sits third rather than last because it is the single
thing no competitor demo shows, and because it looks like magic in motion in
a way a list of features never does. If you cut this video to 10 seconds
later, cut the donut, keep the paste.

The closing line is the same sentence as the store listing's short
description, deliberately. Someone who sees the clip and then lands on the
Play page should read the same promise twice.

## Rules for the captions themselves

- **No dashes as clause separators.** Use a period, comma or colon. This is
  the same rule the store copy follows, and it is what keeps the video and
  the listing sounding like one product.
- Two lines maximum per caption, six words a line at most. At thumbnail size
  a third line is unreadable.
- Hold each caption for its full beat. Do not animate word by word: it reads
  as a template and the eye cannot settle.
- **No Apple App Store badge anywhere.** There is no iOS build. The link is
  `play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trimio.app`

## Music and sound

- Pick something with a beat landing near 2.0s so the first cut lands on it.
- No voiceover. Fifteen seconds is too short, and most viewers are muted.
- Every caption must carry the whole message on its own, silent.

## Cutdowns from the same footage

Shoot once, cut three times. These are the same clips in different orders,
no extra filming.

- **6 seconds, for ads.** Hook, then paste-email, then the badge. Nothing else.
- **10 seconds, organic.** Drop the donut beat.
- **30 seconds, Play Store listing video.** Same spine, but hold each screen
  longer and add the cancellation guide beat: "Cancel in two taps, not
  twenty." / "Kündigen in zwei Taps, nicht zwanzig."

The Play Store video is worth doing while the footage is fresh. It uses your
feature graphic as its thumbnail, which is now on the navy palette, so the
listing finally holds together top to bottom.
